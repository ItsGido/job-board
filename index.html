Services

What we handle

General handyman work around the house — small jobs, done well.

  • Drywall & general repairs

    Patching holes, wall repair, and everyday fix-it jobs around the house.

  • Painting

    Interior and exterior painting, touch-ups, and refresh jobs.

  • Carpentry & mounting

    Shelving, furniture assembly, TV and mirror mounting, minor carpentry.

  • Fixture swaps & small installs

    Hardware, fixtures, weatherstripping, caulking, and other small installs.

What we don't do: licensed plumbing, electrical, or HVAC work, or larger renovation projects. We'll be upfront if a job needs a licensed specialist and can point you in the right direction.
